The range matters more than the number. The bar shows the 80% interval: the outcome lands inside it about four times in five. The white tick is the point projection. Measured prediction spread is roughly half the spread of real outcomes, so a single number understates how high a good week can go — two players with the same projection and different ranges are not the same bet.

Projections run low, by a known amount. Measured against real 2025 results using a model that never saw that season (weeks 6/10/14): mean error —. Some of this is inherent — the model minimises average error, which pulls estimates toward the middle of a right-skewed distribution — and a log-transform correction already removes roughly 40% of it. Treat projections as conservative, especially at the top.
