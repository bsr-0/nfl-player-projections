Ranked by value over replacement. Replacement level is the last starter at each position given your settings (teams × starters), taken from these same projections. Sorting by raw points instead puts six quarterbacks in the top ten — accurate scoring, poor draft advice, because you start one QB and the 13th-best is nearly as good as the 5th.

Range is a 50% interval, not a floor and ceiling in the usual sense: the outcome lands inside it about half the time. It was widened from a ~87% interval whose median width was 206% of the projection — wide enough to be uninformative. Season projection is genuinely uncertain; MAE is roughly 43 points against a mean near 100.

Players without a projection are omitted. Anyone whose prior season had fewer than 6 games is excluded deliberately: measured over 849 such player-seasons, no baseline beats predicting zero, and the obvious fix (per-game rate × 17) is worse than predicting zero. A number here would read as a claim the data cannot support.
